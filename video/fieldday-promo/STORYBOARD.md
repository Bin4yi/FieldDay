# FieldDay promo — storyboard

Single composition (`index.html`), 132 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps. Arc: problem (screens) → reveal → how it works
(say → referee → battles → quests → online → offline) → payoff (time outside) → logo.
Music: code-composed bed, 120 BPM (bar = 2 s); every cut lands on a bar or half bar.
Rules cited are from `hyperframes-animation` (kinetic type slam, counter roll, sticker pop, slow push).
