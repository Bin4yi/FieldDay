# Demo video script (about 2 minutes)

Film outside, in a park, in daylight. Phone on a backpack, 3–4 m away.

1. **Hook (0:00–0:10)** — Wide shot: two friends on grass, phone on a bag. Voice over:
   "Say any game. Play it outside. The phone is the referee."
2. **Airplane mode (0:10–0:20)** — Close-up: swipe down, turn on airplane mode. Show the green
   "No signal · Still works" sticker on Home.
3. **Say a game (0:20–0:40)** — Tap the coral mic: "highest throw battle, 3 rounds, 2 players".
   Volt thinks, the card appears, the referee reads the rules.
4. **Field Check (0:40–0:55)** — The checklist turns green one by one; calibration ("Ama, stand
   still for two seconds"). Tap to place the throw line.
5. **Play (0:55–1:25)** — Throws with the skeleton and ball trail; the big number; "2.40
   metres! Massive!"; Binula's turn: "the score to beat is 2.40 metres". Winner + Volt cheer.
6. **Results (1:25–1:35)** — "You played 6 minutes. Screen time: 4%." Highlight clip plays.
7. **Ghost (1:35–1:45)** — Show the ghost QR; a second phone (also offline) scans it and the
   referee says "Binula's ghost threw 2.6 metres. You need more!"
8. **Boss Raid (1:45–1:55)** — Quick cuts: jumping at Thunder Rock, "FREEZE!", HP bar empties.
9. **Close (1:55–2:05)** — Hero banner. "Gemma on the phone. Works with no signal. Go outside."

then the Boost Mode Realtime voice referee, then turning Wi-Fi off mid-game and the phone voice
